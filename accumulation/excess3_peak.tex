\begin{abstract}
Version~1 of this note reported that, on four diffusively coupled logistic
maps, the hybrid $\excess=0.6\,\mathrm{Syn}+0.4\,\mathrm{Surp}$ is largest
near period-doubling accumulation. That location was produced by
nested-recd~0.2.2, which flattened joint tuples when it counted the
entropies inside $\mathrm{Syn}$. This version keeps the generator, the
seeds, the grid, and the weights, and counts joint tuples
(nested-recd~0.2.3). On windows of 13 symbols the six-seed mean is largest
at the right edge of the grid, $r=3.95$, where
$\excess=2.897\pm 0.006$. At $r=3.5675$ the mean is
$2.383\pm 0.0004$. Surprise still peaks in the accumulation band,
$5.936\pm 0.004$, on $4.11\pm 0.02$ joint tuples, and the full-block
residual there remains $0.0003\pm 0.0001$. Synergy is
$0.0137\pm 0.0021$ at $r=3.5675$ and $1.782\pm 0.010$ at $r=3.95$.
The short-window maximum is therefore the chaotic rise of $\mathrm{Syn}$.
Leave-one-seed maxima all fall at $r=3.95$. For windows of length $78$ and
longer the argmax returns to the accumulation band. At $r=3.85$ the
full-block residual remains $0.192\pm 0.002$. The legacy estimator
reproduces every archived window-13 mean of version~1.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

A critical transition is often described through the growth of variance
and lag-1 autocorrelation \citep{Scheffer2009}. An ordinal reading asks a
different question: whether the joint order of several series is being
reorganized \citep{BandtPompe2002,Padilla2026framework}. In that reading,
Systemic Tau summarizes pairwise rank concordance, and a discrete clock is
built from nested ordinal conjunctions. The third level of the clock is
the residual of the joint ordinal law beyond pairwise margins. The hybrid
$\excess$ was specified so that a short window could be scored when that
residual is costly to compute \citep{Padilla2026excess3,Padilla2026nested}.
The weights $0.6$ and $0.4$ are a declared design choice. They were not
fitted to the logistic suite.

On the published cascade of diffusively coupled logistic maps,
the pooled-count proxy is higher at $r=3.85$ than at $r=3.30$, and its
grid maximum lies near the accumulation point, at about $2.38$
\citep{Padilla2026framework}. Version~1 of this note reproduced that
maximum and read it as a low-diversity orbit. The reading of the support
is kept. The location of the hybrid maximum is not: it was an output of
the flattened count inside $\mathrm{Syn}$.

The decomposition uses the published generator, the published seeds, and
the published weights, on a grid refined through the accumulation band.
Joint tuples are counted with nested-recd~0.2.3. Three comparisons stay
fixed. The first splits the hybrid into $\mathrm{Syn}$ and
$\mathrm{Surp}$. The second asks whether the pairwise maximum-entropy law
already produces the score. The third asks how the ordering of stations
changes when the window is lengthened, and whether a period-3 window
follows the chaotic stations. $\Respair$ is reported beside the proxy.

\section{Estimators}
\label{sec:estimators}

Let $S_t\in\{0,\ldots,5\}^4$ be the joint Bandt--Pompe symbol of order
$m=3$ and delay $1$ at time $t$ \citep{BandtPompe2002}. In a window of
length $w$, write $p$ for the empirical law of the joint symbol and
$p_i$ for its marginals. Total correlation, in bits, is
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{TC}
=\sum_{i=1}^{4} H(p_i)-H(p).
\end{equation}
The pairwise summary used by the proxy is $(4-1)$ times the mean pairwise
mutual information. Synergy in this construction is the nonnegative excess
of total correlation over that summary,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:syn}
\mathrm{Syn}
=\bigl[\mathrm{TC}-(d-1)\,\overline{I}_{\mathrm{pair}}\bigr]_+,
\qquad d=4.
\end{equation}
Surprise retains the positive part of the log-ratio against independence,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:surp}
\mathrm{Surp}
=\sum_{s} p(s)\,
\bigl[\log_2 p(s)-\log_2 p_{\mathrm{ind}}(s)\bigr]_+,
\end{equation}
with $p_{\mathrm{ind}}(s)=\prod_i p_i(s_i)$ and the marginal probability
floored at $10^{-9}$ in the published implementation. The hybrid is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:excess}
\excess=0.6\,\mathrm{Syn}+0.4\,\mathrm{Surp}.
\end{equation}
These names are local to the proxy. They are not the synergy and
redundancy atoms of a partial information decomposition
\citep{Williams2010}.

The strong residual is a different functional. Let $P^{(2)}$ be the
maximum-entropy law with the same pairwise margins as $p$, obtained by
iterative proportional fitting. Then
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:res}
\Respair
=D_{\mathrm{KL}}\bigl(p\,\Vert\,P^{(2)}\bigr),
\end{equation}
in bits. A positive value of $\excess$ does not imply a positive value of
$\Respair$. Surprise is large for a joint that is far from independence,
including a joint that pairs already explain.

\section{Protocol}
\label{sec:protocol}

The system is the diffusively coupled logistic family used for the
published cascade \citep{Kaneko1992,Padilla2026framework}. Four
coordinates obey
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:map}
\begin{aligned}
f_i(t)&=r\,x_i(t)\bigl(1-x_i(t)\bigr),\\
x_i(t+1)
&=(1-\varepsilon)\,f_i(t)
+\varepsilon\,\overline{f}_{-i}(t)
+\xi_i(t),
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
with the mean $\overline{f}_{-i}$ taken over the other three logistic
images, $\varepsilon=0.05$, and $\xi_i(t)$ independent Gaussian noise of
standard deviation $0.003$. Each coordinate is then clipped to $[0,1]$.
The initial condition is uniform on $[0.2,0.8]$. Four hundred steps are
discarded and $2200$ steps are retained. The seed of realization $k$ at
parameter $r$ is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:seed}
10000+\lfloor 10000\,r\rfloor+17k,
\qquad k=0,\ldots,5.
\end{equation}
After the embedding, each series contains $2198$ joint symbols.

The parameter grid is the 32-point cascade grid, union a uniform grid of
19 values on $[3.545,3.590]$, restricted to $[3.05,3.98]$. Forty-nine
distinct values remain. The conventional accumulation value of the
logistic family is $r_\infty=3.5699456$
\citep{Feigenbaum1978,Feigenbaum1983}. The nearest grid point is
$r=3.569946$. The period-3 window is represented by $r=3.828427$.

For windows of length $13$ the hybrid is the mean over every consecutive
window, which is the published abundance. For every other length in
$\{6,8,10,13,20,26,40,52,78,104,156,208,312\}$
the same mean is taken on about $160$ evenly spaced windows, the stride
being the smallest integer that yields at most that count. The full-block
scores use all $2198$ symbols as one window. Means and standard errors
below are taken across the six seeds. The standard error uses the sample
standard deviation with divisor $5$.

Two Monte Carlo controls use the invariant joint of each series. Eighty
sequences of length $13$ are drawn independently from $p$, and eighty from
$P^{(2)}$, with the random generator seeded by the series seed plus $901$.
A local control repeats the comparison inside $36$ evenly spaced windows
of length $13$, with four draws from that window's own $p$ and four from
its pairwise projection. The film strip in Figure~\ref{fig:film} is one
realization per station: the seed whose mean number of distinct 13-symbol
tuples is closest to the six-seed mean, and, inside that series, a run of
16 symbols whose support is closest to the series mean.

With \texttt{legacy\_pooled\_counting} the window-13 means at $r=3.30$
and $r=3.85$ agree with the version~1 archive to the stored digits,
$1.757509$ and $1.863299$. The largest absolute gap on the $294$ series
is zero at the printed precision. The joint-tuple means at those stations
are $2.210069$ and $2.802431$. At every station the corrected means
satisfy $0.6\,\mathrm{Syn}+0.4\,\mathrm{Surp}=\excess$.

\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

\subsection{The short-window maximum is at the grid edge}
\label{sec:band}

Figure~\ref{fig:decomp} shows the three scores along the grid.
Table~\ref{tab:stations} lists the stations used below. The largest
six-seed mean of $\excess$ is $2.897\pm 0.006$ at $r=3.95$.
Every leave-one-seed maximum falls at that same station.
At $r=3.5675$ the mean is $2.383\pm 0.0004$, and at the neighboring
station $r=3.566168$ it is $2.383\pm 0.0004$. At the nearest grid point
to $r_\infty$ the mean is $2.375\pm 0.007$. Across the 33 stations in
$[3.544,3.595]$ every mean lies between $2.344$ and $2.383$. The band is
still a plateau. It sits about $0.51$ bits below the grid edge.

At $r=3.5675$ the two pieces are
$\mathrm{Syn}=0.0137\pm 0.0021$ and
$\mathrm{Surp}=5.936\pm 0.004$. Their weighted contributions are
$0.0082$ and $2.374$ bits. Developed chaos at $r=3.85$ has
$\excess=2.802\pm 0.005$, so the accumulation station lies $0.420$ bits
below it. The Surp term contributes $+0.566$ bits to that difference and
the Syn term contributes $-0.986$ bits. Along the 49 grid means, the
correlation of $\excess$ with $\mathrm{Surp}$ is $-0.204$, with
$\mathrm{Syn}$ it is $0.643$, and with the number of distinct tuples it
is $0.375$. The largest Syn on the grid is $1.782\pm 0.010$ at $r=3.95$,
which is also where $\excess$ is largest.

\subsection{The joint support collapses}
\label{sec:support}

Figure~\ref{fig:support} places the support next to the block residual.
At $r=3.5675$ a window of 13 symbols contains
$4.11\pm 0.02$ distinct joint tuples, visited $3.20$ times on average,
with modal mass $0.304$. At $r=3.30$ the same window contains
$11.39\pm 0.05$ tuples. At $r=3.85$ it contains $12.62\pm 0.02$, visited
$1.04$ times, with modal mass $0.098$. The grid maximum,
$r=3.95$, contains $12.79\pm 0.02$ tuples.

Figure~\ref{fig:film} shows sixteen consecutive symbols from the
representative realization at each of those three stations. At
$r=3.5675$ the segment is the cycle of four joint symbols
$1,2,3,4$, repeated four times. The integers are the order of first
appearance in the segment. They label distinct 4-tuples of permutation
indices; they are not comparable across panels, and they are not
themselves the Bandt--Pompe ranks. At $r=3.30$ the segment uses eleven
joint symbols. At $r=3.85$ it uses thirteen.

\subsection{Pairs reproduce the stationary score}
\label{sec:pairs}

Table~\ref{tab:controls} compares the accumulation station with developed
chaos. At $r=3.5675$, draws from the empirical joint $p$ score
$2.161\pm 0.033$, and draws from $P^{(2)}$ score $2.174\pm 0.025$.
The ratio of the pairwise score to the sliding score is $0.912$.
At $r=3.85$ the same ratio is $0.942$, and at the grid maximum
$r=3.95$ the two draw means are $2.743\pm 0.014$ and $2.736\pm 0.015$.
The pairwise projection accounts for the excess3 of independent draws
from the invariant joint at each of these stations. The sliding window
exceeds the independent draws from $p$ by $0.221\pm 0.033$ bits at
$r=3.5675$. Those windows occupy $4.11$ tuples, while the independent
draws from the same $p$ occupy $5.54$.

Inside single windows the two gaps agree with each other. At $r=3.5675$
the gap against the window's own pairwise projection is
$0.225\pm 0.005$, and the gap against draws from that window's own $p$
is $0.206\pm 0.006$. At $r=3.85$ the gaps are $0.340\pm 0.010$ and
$0.325\pm 0.013$. At $r=3.95$ they are $0.365\pm 0.009$ and
$0.366\pm 0.008$. The short window does not separate a residual beyond
pairs from the resampling fluctuation of a 13-symbol table.

\subsection{Long windows return the maximum to the band}
\label{sec:window}

Figure~\ref{fig:windows} extends the hybrid from length 6 to length 312.
At $r=3.5675$ the mean is $2.301$ at length 6, $2.383$ at length 13, and
$2.375\pm 0.008$ at length 312. At that last length the accumulation
series still occupies only $6.55$ joint symbols. At $r=3.85$ the mean is
$2.447$ at length 6, $2.802$ at length 13, $1.964$ at length 104, and
$1.135\pm 0.011$ at length 312, where the window occupies $199.5$ symbols.
The argmax of the six-seed curve is $r=3.95$ for lengths $6$ through $52$,
except length $8$, whose argmax is $r=3.90$. From length $78$ through
$156$ the argmax is $r=3.566168$. At lengths $208$ and $312$ it is
$r=3.5575$. Lengthening the window creates the accumulation maximum.
It does not preserve the short-window ordering.

The period-3 station $r=3.828427$ follows the chaotic curve. Its
window-13 hybrid is $2.723\pm 0.007$, its support is $12.56\pm 0.02$
tuples, its synergy is $1.582\pm 0.009$, and its block residual is
$0.194\pm 0.005$. A periodic window is not, by itself, the accumulation
plateau.

Period~2 behaves in a third way. By length 312 its joint support has
saturated at 32 symbols and $\excess$ has leveled at $1.248$. The
period-8 station $r=3.54409$ is inside the high band of surprise:
$\excess=2.381\pm 0.002$ and the short window holds $4.20\pm 0.09$ tuples.

\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

The support reading survives the recount. Near accumulation the orbit of
the joint symbol lives on about four tuples. Those tuples are far from an
independent product, so $\mathrm{Surp}$ is large, and the full-block
$\Respair$ is $0.0003$ at the reported precision. Developed chaos widens
the support, lowers $\mathrm{Surp}$, and is where the block residual is
about $0.19$. Synergy, as defined by equation~\eqref{eq:syn}, is small on
the accumulation plateau and is about $1.7$ bits in developed chaos. On
windows of length $13$ that term sets the location of the hybrid maximum.

A reading that takes the peak of short-window $\excess$ as the place where
ordinal structure beyond pairs is strongest now points at developed chaos.
A reading that takes the collapse of joint support, or the peak of
$\mathrm{Surp}$, points at the accumulation band. The two readings
separate once $\mathrm{Syn}$ is the joint-tuple quantity. They met in
version~1 because the flattened count kept $\mathrm{Syn}$ below $0.12$
everywhere. The long-window hybrid agrees with the support reading: from
length $78$ the argmax is back inside the band, and the full block is
largest at $r=3.54409$.

Two smaller facts keep the statement inside this ensemble. First,
consecutive windows at $r=3.5675$ are more concentrated than independent
draws from the invariant joint, by $0.221\pm 0.033$ bits. The local
13-symbol gaps against $P^{(2)}$ and against the window's own $p$ agree,
so that concentration is not a resolved residual beyond pairs. Second, one
grid point inside the band, $r=3.582612$, has a block-residual mean of
$0.068$ with standard error $0.059$. Isolated seeds can move a single
station. They do not move the six-seed maximum of the residual, which
remains at $r=3.80$.

The scope is the generator in equation~\eqref{eq:map}: four coordinates,
coupling $0.05$, noise level $0.003$, embedding order $3$, and the six
seeds in equation~\eqref{eq:seed}. The weights in equation~\eqref{eq:excess}
are the published weights. The paper does not refit them. The result does
not derive the operational concordance bands used elsewhere in the
framework, and it does not transfer either maximum to other couplings,
other dimensions, or series measured in the field.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

On this coupled logistic cascade, joint-tuple $\excess$ on windows of
length $13$ is largest at $r=3.95$. The accumulation band is where the
joint symbol repeats about four tuples and $\mathrm{Surp}$ is largest,
and where the full-block residual is quiet. $\mathrm{Syn}$ is the term
that rises in developed chaos and, at this window length, sets the hybrid
maximum. The period-3 window follows that chaotic regime. A longer window
moves the hybrid maximum back into the accumulation band.
